# random_valid

2500 uniformly-ish random teams whose every mon is fully legal in FireRed: verdict `ok` for all 2500
(0 encodable, 0 invalid). Generated by `sim/tools/gen_random_teams.py`; ids `rv_1` .. `rv_2500`,
`source: random_valid`, `tags: ["random", "valid"]`, `format: singles`, `level_cap` = the team's highest level.

Regenerate (deterministic, from `sim/`):

```
python3 tools/gen_random_teams.py --mode valid --count 2500 --seed 1 --out teams/random_valid.jsonl
python3 tools/teams.py check teams/random_valid.jsonl --quiet     # random_valid.jsonl: 2500 teams: 2500 ok, 0 encodable, 0 invalid
```

Legality data is pulled from `build/teamcheck` (`--species`, `--items`, `--abilities`, `--moves SPECIES LEVEL`,
cached per (species, level)), so the legal move pool is exactly the simulator's own `Sim_CanLearnMove` rule.

## Distributions

Team level:

| quantity | distribution |
|---|---|
| team size | 70% six mons; 30% uniform over 1..5 |

Per mon (independent draws, Python `random.Random(seed)`):

| field | distribution |
|---|---|
| species | uniform over the 386 real species (`teamcheck --species` minus the `?` OLD_UNOWN rows; `UNOWN` itself included, no letter forms) |
| level | mixture: 20% uniform 1..20, 30% uniform 21..50, 30% uniform 51..80, 20% uniform 81..100 |
| moves | count 4/3/2/1 with weights 0.80/0.10/0.06/0.04, capped by the size of the legal list; sampled without replacement from `teamcheck --moves SPECIES LEVEL` |
| item | 40% `NONE`, else uniform over the 252 holdable items from `teamcheck --items` (balls, mails, TMs/HMs, berries, hold items, medicine...) |
| ability | uniform over the species' abilities from `teamcheck --abilities` (written by name) |
| nature | uniform over the 25 natures |
| IVs | each uniform 0..31 |
| EVs | 50% all zero; 25% random legal spread (total uniform 0..510, split at 5 uniform cut points, rejected while any stat > 255); 25% "competitive" (two distinct stats 252, a third stat 4 or 6, chosen uniformly) |
| happiness | 30% uniform 0..255, else 70 |
| fateful | `true` for MEW and DEOXYS (required for obedience), absent otherwise |
| ot / nickname | defaults (`player`, species name) |

Stat order is the game's: HP, Atk, Def, Spe, SpA, SpD.

Empirical (seed 1): team sizes 1:146 2:148 3:145 4:138 5:159 6:1764; level buckets 1-20 20.0%, 21-50 29.5%,
51-80 30.0%, 81-100 20.5%; move counts 1:644 2:839 3:1388 4:9937 (the extra 1-move mons are Smeargle and
low-level mons with tiny legal lists); item NONE 40.0%; EVs zero 49.8% / random 24.1% / competitive 26.1%;
happiness 70 in 70.4%; 79 Mew/Deoxys, all fateful; all 386 species appear.

## Extending

`gen_random_teams.py` keeps the per-mode logic in the `MODES` registry (`gen_mon` function + source/tags/id
prefix); the level / IV / EV / happiness / team-size samplers are shared. An `--mode encodable` can be added by
registering a second mon generator there.
