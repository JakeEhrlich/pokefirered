hey ho i hit 1800s with another new articuno team, this time with an articuno lead. i am posting to share the team BUT ALSO i am looking to gain some insight and input to futher optimize the team, because i think it has potential but it needs some tweaking to fully realize its potential.

Articuno @ Leftovers
Ability: Pressure
EVs: 40 HP / 248 SpA / 220 Spe
Timid Nature
IVs: 2 Atk / 30 SpA / 30 Spe
- Ice Beam
- Hidden Power [Fire]
- Toxic
- Haze

heres what you do vs leads:

ice beam ice beam ice beam. if they DD, haze. if after 1st DD they DD again u got rid of their boosts. if after 1st DD they click slide, or if they slide turn 1, u outspeed with dug and kill 100% of the time with ice beam chip. sometimes they try to be cute with it and click focus punch. sometimes they click pursuit. sometimes they think youre not staying in and switch to skarm. either way somethings getting stung with stab ice beam with a chance to freeze.

hp fire hp fire hp fire. if they mash, they kill; bring in dug for the revenge. if they mash and miss, hp fire again. if they agil, uh oh, go suicune and surf them down. although idk who leads lead agil gross but it has happened before.

getting rid of tar and meta enables a jirachi sweep, so trading cuno for them is a worthy trade.

ice beam once, then switch to jira and start pressuring with tbolt. we switch to jira after first ice beam to not eat a toxic.

i go back and forth on what to do with zapdos leads. cuno tanks one tbolt from all zapdos sets with the given investments, and u can snap back with an ice beam that brings zapdos into the red. this enables cune, but youre also down a ground immune. if youre running heal bell on the cuno, i think you should just switch to dol t1.

toxic if u have it, otherwise go jira, match a boost, then trade down with tbolt.

sometimes u outspeed and kill with ice beam, sometimes they outspeed and u drop to rock slide. up to you if you wanna take the risk. usually i just switch to dol or cune, the risk just isnt worth it.

click toxic if u have it, otherwise just hp fire. if their lovely kiss misses then their jynx is compromised; if kiss lands then go tar and trade. we dont mind sand being cleared if they bring in dug for revenge, so whatevs.
