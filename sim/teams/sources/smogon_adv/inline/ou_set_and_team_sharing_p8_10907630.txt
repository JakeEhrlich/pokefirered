Smeargle @ Salac Berry
Ability: Own Tempo
Jolly Nature
- Endure

Endure is a cool option on lead Smeargle. It guarantees Smeargle a turn to set up Spikes against physical attackers, and if confronted by a Special Attacker, you'll get two turns guaranteed(bar crits and full paras). The moveset is pretty customizable because Smeargle has a plethora of viable options. The EVs are flexible as well. You can choose to outrun Aero and Jolteon at +1 or drop the speed down to Dugtrio range to get some extra bulk or Att for a stronger Explosion.
