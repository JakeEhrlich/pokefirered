Hey! Today i bring a cool little Raikou set that might interest some people!

116 defense EVs mean that the standard bulky attacker Metagross does a max of 94.7% damage with Earthquake, while full 252+ atk metagross also STILL doesn't ohko with max 99.4 or .6 % damage! Tho that one u can die to sand after, still it's fine.

The beauty of this spread is that it keeps Raikou speedier than base 100s and pretty powerful, while punishing people who use their Gross as an offensive Raikou check. Some games this will give your Tar or another DDer a much easier time to setup and sweep, or maybe even Kingdra since it won't be able to get Boomed!

Fill the last move as you see the need to. Crunch for Gar, Roar for other CMers and Stuff, etc. Raikou is very customizable and that's another beauty of this mon.

Have a nice one!
