### post 11068532 author=Johnald url=https://www.smogon.com/forums/threads/set-dump-team-dump-and-previously-general-metagame-discussion.3648620/page-9#post-11068532
Have not played the game much. But had the chance to ladder with an idea I had for physical defensive rest Skarmory.

https://pokepast.es/4d8b85aa762ce758

CB Metagross is insanely strong in ADV but is gatekept by both Magneton and Dugtrio.
This team takes advantage of this by revenge trapping both with Gardevoir.
If Magneton is eliminated, Skarmory becomes strong against physical threats.
If Dugtrio is eliminated, Blissey's job is much easier.

Gard is ev'd to live adamant EQ + struggle in sand allowing you to stall it out with Wish + Protect (88% chance of dodging both crits).
Gard has enough speed to outspeed and firepunch Magneton twice bypassing twave shenanigans.
If they do not have either trapper, gard still functions nicely as a wish passer for CB Meta/SkarmDol.
Torment and Trace often find more utility than you'd think. Tormenting Setup Sweepers or Endpert. Tracing pressure, natcure, volt absorb etc.

I think you could even try the p2 route and include more Pokémon vulnerable to Dugtrio.

anyways, this is my favorite adv team for a couple of reasons, and i can break it down in a messy and freeflowing way.
Lead Zapdos is the best lead in ADV, because it beats everything, except for tyranitar. Lead Zapdos when paired with Offensive Swampert is basically the best opening you can possibly get - lead twave zap favorably trades with everything, except tar where you bp to pert and rip a fat hydro and you're also winning off of this.
Offensive swampert as sole rock resist is illegal, unless you tech very heavily to allow it. Here, we do not both with such things. We use registeel.
In 2026, people use Tyranitar + Aerodactyl and just hurl the tar into swampert, suicide for damage, and then expect aero to farm. Having a backup rock resist is fantastic for this trend.

Our offense comes from Aerodactyl and Tyranitar. Clicking slide over and over breaks the vast majority of defensive squads, and versus the other ones you can figure it out with dug and boom and stuff. I like Adamant Dugtrio + DDTar here because we suicide DDtar into their swampert, and then dug trap it(ada dug picks it off from great ranges) and then aerodactyl actually gets to go in. However, if they have a second rock resist(meta, steel, jira etc) they can get eaten by dugtrio, so we aren't left stranded without enough muscle to win the game.

Adamant dugtrio is excellent at picking off Celebi and Jirachi, so zapdos is very able to get it angles to pick kills.
This team is very flexible -> you have Generally Great openings versus everything, and enough checks to stuff that if you preserve hp on your mons its easy to convert a health lead into winning the game.
Aero is hp invest and not max speed because speed tie is worthless when u have two rock resists + u know how to play + ur zap twaves enemy jolt + the hp is important for living surfs from cune and milo, we rely pretty heavily on aero in stall matchups like that.

you may recognise this is reminiscent of the beloved M Dragon four choice bands team. I would be lying if I said I was inspired by it, but then after using it to qualify for ladder tournament and playing some various other tour stuff, I did notice the resemblance. Notably, it's a more offensive swampert and zapdos set, and registeel allows for more resilience(in place of his Choice Band Metagross). I am very aware that my capitalisation of pokemon names is inconsistent. I do not care.
The point I'm getting at is that it retreads his steps a bit -> we recognise that twave zapdos is a great lead, and flexible check to stuff, and it messes with cune for your rocks so you arent ripped by lead cune, and it likes dugtrio and they're sufficient together, and idk like. there's a lot of reason to use these guys all together. I think that the usage of Registeel allows for better spikes resilience -> Offensive Swampert makes us better into enemy skarmory, we have a generally frailer team and can play more fast and loose without losing to the likes of DDmence or Any Special Attacker Ever.

If you’ve played me on ladder this past week, you’ve seen me toying with these two sets. Figured I’d share them here. Don’t get too greedy, though. I’m not going to explain the EVs for you.

Regirock @ Leftovers
Ability: Clear Body
EVs: 252 HP / 184 Atk / 72 Spe
Adamant Nature
IVs: 30 SpD / 30 Spe
- Curse
- Focus Punch
- Rock Slide
- Hidden Power [Bug]

I got in a bit of a debate with SpamOn on the vr thread about regirock being an awkward fit on spikes. You can read what I said there, but the gist is that standard regirock is weak against swampert and naturally walled by claydol. One solution is choice band, but lefties regirock has such great utility against aero, snorlax, mence and gyara as well as staying power against blissey. CB is not for every team. This is another potential solution.

Focus punch + slide + bug, when paired with spikes, hits just about everything pretty hard. Over time, it’ll break anything except for rest suicune, milotic, and wish jirachi. The damage with these three moves is still a little bit lackluster, though, especially against Skarmory which is a massive threat to spikes teams in particular. So instead of boom or superpower in the last slot, I’ve opted for curse.

Curse punishes Skarmory really hard if it tries to protect or drill peck to cancel your focus punch. If a spike is down, classic physical checks like swampert are not comfortable switching in to +1 focus punch at all, and focus punch resists get decked by slide or bug. This is probably best paired with heal bell so that you can take a toxic from skarm and keep it pushing. Curse also doubles as a way to check snorlax pretty reliably since you don’t have boom or superpower anymore. This set plays like a bkc tyranitar that is much sturdier vs physical attackers in exchange for not helping against jirachi and raikou and not bringing sand. You just need to make sure you have a good plan for mono waters. This  Dragonite set is one idea for that.

Dragonite @ Leftovers
Ability: Inner Focus
EVs: 236 HP / 104 Atk / 68 Def / 100 Spe
Adamant Nature
IVs: 30 SpA / 30 SpD / 30 Spe
- Hidden Power [Flying]
- Earthquake
- Focus Punch
- Dragon Dance

Finally I’ve found a set that doesn’t feel outclassed by mence. In most matchups, this is a worse salamence (although still pretty serviceable). But this set really shines against common stall teams right now. Bold cune and Milotic without ice beam aren’t good against this set at all. This also punishes non-ice beam blissey which has been common for a while and is always hard to beat.

Focus punch has some other nice use cases as well. It lets Dragonite be a much better early-mid game dugtrio punish than dd mence ever was. It’s great coverage for protox skarm and can also hit drill peck sets if you play well. The nuke option against tar, blissey, and snorlax is generally very helpful.

I think dd + focus punch can very easily be used with hp rock over hp flying. Other wacky options like fire blast, rock tomb, wing attack, hp ghost, sub, twave and heal bell are probably also usable. Have fun!
