### post 10927956 author=SpamOn url=https://www.smogon.com/forums/threads/set-dump-team-dump-and-previously-general-metagame-discussion.3648620/page-8#post-10927956
Here are some fun (and hopefully good) teams. I Reached 1800 on the ladder with all of these. Many did even better than this.

: Aero + Offmie SpikeOff
Pretty generic Aero Spikes Offense. This team has been fire though - pretty similar to this but plays at a slightly slower pace. This 6 has probably been used loads in various combinations. If I will say one thing, it's that BoahTar is amazing - so much more consistent than DDTar on this kind of team and an amazing Modest OffMie partner in terms of punishing Bliss and bluffing Suit to Kill Gengar.

: EndPert-Gyara-Hera MagOff
Maybe my favourite team I have ever made: a cross between a Slop team and a Magneton Offense. This team really changed my opinion on Gyarados. Twave Gyara is amazing at Paralysing things like Aero, Zap, Gar and Mence which helps enable Heracross. It can sweep in some games and has amazing defensive utility. It's just not a DDMence replacement due to its coverage issues so keep this in mind.
You could also run Sub > Brick Break on Hera here if you wanted to lean into the Paraspam and be better vs Dug.

: Vape-Champ MixOff
I am full on Vape is OU believer at this point. I think this team is a good example of some of the stuff it can enable through SubPassing. This team performed really well for me on the ladder (getting to 1850+) and can end games very quickly if you position the right mon correctly. I do think you need to get the hits in with Machamp early game to break some teams though, especially so you don't get spiked up on, and so perhaps not the best for your blood pressure if the Cross Chops fly left.

: Blaziken-P2 Offence
Although generally terrible, Blaziken's offensive profile does offer something unique to P2 teams, given it's a mixed attacker that can be a strong opener vs many balance (big5) TSS structures, against which many of Pory's other typical partners can struggle. It also gives the team a Wisp switch in vs Gar/Molt, mons which often trouble this kind of team (that is about as much as I can say about Blaziken's defensive utility).
This team is built around using Blaziken, SuperBi and CBMeta to beat down on skarm and other flyers to enable DDMence or Hera to sweep. P2's presence is greatly appreciated here, not only to trap Dug but also to answer Mence and, in a pinch, Aero.

: Dual Spin + CB Mence
Probably my first original stally team that doesn't suck. We have double spin here with HPGhost Forre aiming to spike early and trade with a potential gengar to enable Rest Donphan spinning later on (obviously Forre can be used conventially in Garless MUs). CB Mence provides the team with speed control/revenge killing + fighter check and Sub-CM Rachi is a direct win con. Bliss + Vape provide Wish and Aroma support to give the team immense longevity.
One could perhaps consider using Boom > Rest on Forre here to be a trader/progress maker, given we can wish it up if needed and have a second spinner.

: Chubby-Cham Balance
I wanted to make a team in which Medicham did not feel hideously inconsistent. This team is probably more of a proof of concept, though I think I at least somewhat succeeded (IMO).
Bulky Recover Medi + SuitTar is an amazing breaking core vs fat. MixedZap is great at enabling Medi by forcing Bliss in and makes direct progress vs Celebi teams which the prior two can struggle against. Wish Rachi + DefMie provide the team with support and longevity whilst also spreading para to help enable our slow Medi. Offgon acts as another rock resist and a cleaner which benefits from the para spreading and Medi's wall-breaking. Bulky Medi is a surprisingly good BKC Tar Switch in given it resists Slide and FP, and this helps us get away with using OffGon here.
One problem for the team was that Medi can get statused vs Bliss if you aren't careful, basically killing it. Would probably lead to more issues than it solves but that is something to think about. I would love to be able to fit Wish + Heal Bell on this team and still retain the Medi + SuitTar core and have a Celebi punish. There probably is a way.
