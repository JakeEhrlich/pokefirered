Hello! Welcome to my very funny team dump.
This dump encompasses most of my teams from the past seven-or-so months, hopefully the good ones.
I've organised it into 3 sections, each section separated by a pizza:
Section 1: the highlights;
Section 2: decent teams I didn't bring to tours;
Section 3: decent teams I did bring to tours.
Every team has a blurb. I thought they would be fun to write.
I also listed any tournament sets I brought them to.
I've omitted some teams I used in tournaments for being too derivative or too garbage.
But the vast majority of teams you've seen me use are here.

Big shoutouts to: Mezzoloth, Giraffe, Fruhdazi, Muteki, UDM, pzwrd, Zetts.
Slightly smaller thanks to Darth Tyros.
