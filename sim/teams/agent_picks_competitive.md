# agent_picks_competitive

60 Level-100 teams (50 singles across OU / UU / NU / Ubers, 10 doubles) built from scratch by the agent as an
experienced Generation 3 competitive player, one per line below. `source: agent`, tags
`["persona:competitive", <tier>, <archetype>]`. All teams are `ok` under `tools/teams.py check`
(FireRed-legal movesets: no Emerald tutor moves such as the elemental punches on Gengar / Jirachi, Self-Destruct or
Sleep Talk on most users). Hidden Power types are encoded in the IVs (all 30/31, power 70).
Generated by a script that holds the team data in Python and emits the JSONL; EV spreads were written in
Showdown order and converted to the game's HP/Atk/Def/Spe/SpA/SpD order.
