# strategies.jsonl - hand-built Generation 3 strategy teams

Generated by `python3 sim/tools/gen_strategy_teams.py` (the teams live in that file as data; `--check` runs the
checker). Every team has `source: "strategy"`, id `strat_<archetype>_<n>`, tags `[archetype, tier?, endgame|midgame, ...]`
and a `notes` line saying who leads and what the win condition is (usable as a play hint).

Intended verdicts: everything `ok` except the teams tagged `not_frlg_legal` (deliberately illegal, reason in notes).
Current result: **136 teams: 135 ok, 1 encodable, 0 invalid**. The only encodable is `strat_fear_2`, the
"as commonly described" level-1 Endure/Endeavor Rattata team, tagged `not_frlg_legal` on purpose.

Levels: 111 endgame teams at L100 (competitive sets, tier tag `ou`/`uu`/`ubers` where relevant), 25 `midgame` teams at
L35-50 built only from Pokemon a FireRed player can have in Kanto (one uses Sevii-island Wobbuffet, tagged `sevii`).
Formats: 123 singles, 13 doubles (`format: "doubles"`, archetypes prefixed `dbl_`).

FRLG legality notes that shaped the sets (all from `build/teamcheck --moves`): no species learns both Endure and
Endeavor in FRLG (Endure is level-up only, no tutor); Sleep Talk exists only as a level-up move (Snorlax, Lapras,
Ursaring, Miltank, Slowbro, Lickitung...); Gengar/Jirachi have no elemental punches; Blissey has no Wish; Snorlax has
no Self-Destruct; Zapdos has no Baton Pass; Seadra has Poison Point (Horsea/Kingdra have Swift Swim); Skill Swap,
Trick, Knock Off, Magic Coat and Helping Hand are TM/level/egg moves on a limited set of species. Things that only
exist in later generations (Focus Sash, Stealth Rock, Trick Room, Cursed Body) are not represented.
